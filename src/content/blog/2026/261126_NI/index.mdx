My new artworks, including for the first time a playful interactive installation and colour pencil drawings, will show alongside 5 other artists in an exhibition I co-curated with Sasha Anikina as part of my [research fellowship](https://soundscale.ac.uk/artistic-research/) with SOUNDSCALE (University of Southampton and [Critical Infrastructure and Image Politics (CIIP)](https://criticalinfrastructures.net/about/) at Winchester School of Art). From The Winchester Gallery, this exhibition will then tour to Borough Road Gallery, London, in February 2027. 

"Technological infrastructures surround us: they help us connect to other people and places, but they also show the barriers of who has access to which services. They set the conditions for how we live, work and play, yet technology is often deployed without, and even against, community consent.

This exhibition asks, what does it mean to be a neighbour to technological infrastructures that are imposed, built and deployed around us? What are some ways to activate agency between people to resist and reshape these conditions for communal needs and foster a sense of belonging as neighbours to one another?

Neighbouring Infrastructures explores the themes of community and infrastructures through multimedia works ranging from sound, installation, sculpture, video and illustration by the artists Angela YT Chan, Alexandra (Sasha) Anikina, Natalie Hyacinth, Net Ember, ZEST Collective and Yadira Sánchez.

This exhibition is part of UKRI-funded project SOUNDSCALE at the University of Southampton.

Please note: The Gallery will be closed from 21 December 2026 to 4 January 2027." 


More details to come about the artworks details and documentation, as well as the accompanying public and student workshop programme.
