I will join other game-makers, artists, scholars, and thinkers to assemble in London for a one-day facilitated symposium on the topic: Games against borders. Together with my collaborator Louis Cook, we will bring our insights from developing ['Sound, Sensors and Surveillance' a Bitsy game by Angela YT Chan and Louis Cook](https://angelaytchan.net/blog/2026/260922_ssslaunch/) to day's sessions that will explore the role of radical games. 

"This event is a sequel to the 2025 symposium “What is the Antifascist Game,” organised by RiVAL: The ReImagining Value Action Lab in partnership with the [Games Transformed Festival](https://www.gamestransformed.org/) for radical games and play, which this year will take place on from October 30 until November 1 and will be dedicated to racial and migrant justice. 

Some key discussions will be: 

- The urgent situation of hardening border control
- Reactionary, far-right and fascistic forces In the UK and beyond are organising to strengthen the border
- Games, the game industries, and gamer culture are far from innocent
- Games can also offer profound opportunities to challenge these systems of domination
- How can games develop a play- and game-ful opposition to bordering?
- How can the power of games to break and transgress borders?
- Borders are neither natural nor inevitable
- Games don’t just recreate the border ideologically, they also do so materially
- Games are among the most important platforms for the radical imagination, for transversal solidarity and for subversive pleasure in the 21st century

This symposium is being hosted by [RiVAL: The Reimagining Value Action Lab](https://reimaginingvalue.ca/) with support from the Games Transformed festival and is being coordinated by [Max Haiven](https://maxhaiven.com/), [Briar Dickey](https://echo.research.vub.be/briar-dickey) and [Nick Koppenhagen](https://nickkoppenhagen.net/)."
